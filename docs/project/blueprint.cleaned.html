Application Features

A consolidated list of user-facing and administrative features for the My Guide web application. This document intentionally contains features only.

VisitorConversationGuidance & action plansEligibilityKnowledge qualityProfile & memoryCredits & paymentsWhatsAppPublic articlesAccountAdministration
01

Visitor and landing experience

Features available before and during first use.

Responsive web experience

Usable on desktop, tablet and mobile browsers with layouts and controls suited to each screen size.

Warm welcome screen

Display a friendly greeting and a clear prompt inviting the visitor to ask a question.

Free-text query input

Allow visitors to enter questions naturally, without selecting a predefined category first.

Example queries

Show optional example topics such as loans, government schemes, jobs, education and business support.

Guest-first access

Allow visitors to submit a query and receive initial guidance without creating an account.

Optional name entry

Provide a small “Set your name” action that opens a simple, skippable name-entry prompt.

02

Conversational guidance

Features that understand the query and guide the user progressively.

Query and intent understanding

Identify the subject, user's goal and intended outcome from natural-language questions.

Immediate initial response

Provide a useful first explanation before requesting additional information.

Dynamic response sections

Select the sections relevant to each query instead of forcing every query into a fixed template.

Progressive choices

Present around three or four relevant choices at a time, with short explanations.

Adaptive follow-up questions

Ask relevant questions based on the user's answers and the information still required.

One-question-at-a-time interaction

Where practical, collect information in small steps instead of presenting a long questionnaire.

Personalised explanations

Adjust language, examples and level of detail to the user's apparent understanding.

Conversation context

Use answers already provided in the current conversation and avoid asking for the same details again.

Ambiguity resolution

Identify unclear requests and offer possible interpretations for the user to select.

Conversation continuation

Allow users to explore a selected topic, return to earlier choices and continue the guidance flow.

03

Information and action-plan features

Dynamic content blocks that can appear according to the user's query.

Definition

Explain what the scheme, service, opportunity, process or subject means.

General information

Present purpose, benefits, key concepts, important conditions and relevant background.

Broader context

Show the parent programme, category, pathway or related options one level above the selected topic.

Beneficiary information

Explain who the scheme, service or opportunity is intended for.

Eligibility criteria

Display relevant eligibility requirements in clear, understandable language.

Personalised eligibility assessment

Use the user's answers to provide a preliminary assessment of likely eligibility.

Documents checklist

List documents and information the user may need to prepare.

Application process

Explain how to apply or access the relevant scheme, service or opportunity.

Step-by-step action plan

Provide an ordered list of practical actions tailored to the user's selected next step.

Official application links

Provide relevant official portals, forms and application pages where available.

Office and location details

Provide relevant office names, addresses, visiting information and directions when reliably available.

Contact information

Provide verified phone numbers, email addresses and official help channels when available.

Fees and deadlines

Show applicable fees, dates, timelines and important deadlines when verified.

Alternative pathways

Suggest other relevant options when the selected option may not fit the user's circumstances.

Save and revisit action plan

Allow signed-in users to access previously generated plans and their supporting information.

04

Eligibility assessment

Features for checking whether an option may apply to the user.

Eligibility question flow

Ask only the questions needed to assess the user's situation against relevant criteria.

Progressive information collection

Collect eligibility details one step at a time and allow users to clarify or correct answers.

Eligibility outcome

Present a clear outcome such as “appears eligible”, “may not be eligible” or “more information needed”.

Reason for outcome

Explain which criteria appear to be met, not met or still require confirmation.

Missing information indicator

Identify the details the user still needs to provide for a more meaningful assessment.

Official confirmation guidance

Direct users to the relevant authority, lender or institution for a final decision.

05

Knowledge quality and trust

Features that help users judge the reliability and limits of guidance.

Authoritative source preference

Prioritise official government, institutional and service-provider sources for important claims.

Source references

Show the sources supporting eligibility, benefits, application steps, fees and deadlines.

Information date

Display source or last-checked dates for information that may change over time.

Verified versus general guidance labels

Distinguish sourced facts from general explanations, estimates and preliminary assessments.

Uncertainty disclosure

Clearly state when information is incomplete, uncertain or needs confirmation.

Incorrect information reporting

Allow users to report incorrect information, outdated links or missing details.

06

User profile, consent and memory

Features for optional identity, saved context and continuity.

Progressive profile creation

Invite users to save their progress after they have received initial value.

One-at-a-time profile prompts

Request name, email address and phone number separately where practical.

Optional information

Allow users to skip optional profile questions and continue using the service.

Non-duplicative prompts

Do not request details the user has already provided and permitted My Guide to retain.

Purpose explanation

Explain why each personal detail is requested and how it will be used.

Consent to save context

Obtain permission before retaining profile details, personal context or conversation progress.

Interest and goal context

Save user-provided interests, goals, occupation or education context and requirements when permitted.

Preference memory

Retain useful user preferences that improve future guidance, subject to user permission.

Previous query history

Let users review their previous questions and generated action plans.

Resume previous activity

Allow users to continue an unfinished query or revisit a pending action plan.

Profile review and correction

Allow users to see and correct their saved personal information.

Data and consent controls

Allow users to withdraw permissions and request deletion of saved personal information.

Relevant return suggestions

When a user returns, show useful suggestions based on permitted context and previous interests.

07

Free credits, pricing and payments

Features for transparent, outcome-based charging.

First two free queries

Provide the first two queries free for each eligible user.

Free-credit balance

Show the user's remaining free queries or credits.

Completion-based charge trigger

Request payment only when the query is sufficiently clear and a useful action plan is ready.

No charge for unresolved queries

Do not charge when material ambiguity remains or a useful action plan cannot be produced.

Price display before payment

Show the exact price and what the user will receive before requesting payment.

Indicative query pricing

Support the proposed ₹2–₹5 price range per completed query, subject to final pricing rules.

Payment status

Show whether a payment is pending, successful or unsuccessful.

Transaction and query history

Allow users to review paid queries, credits used and transaction details.

WhatsApp bonus-credit offer

Optionally award two additional free credits for connecting and verifying WhatsApp, once the offer rules are finalised.

08

WhatsApp experience

Full conversational access through WhatsApp.

Optional WhatsApp connection

Allow users to choose whether to connect their WhatsApp number to their My Guide profile.

Phone-number verification

Verify the number when required to link the WhatsApp channel to the correct user.

Start a query on WhatsApp

Allow users to ask a new question and receive guidance through WhatsApp.

Continue website conversations

Allow users to continue an existing web conversation on WhatsApp when their accounts are linked.

Continue WhatsApp conversations on web

Allow users to resume a linked WhatsApp conversation through the website.

Interactive follow-up flow

Support questions, answers and selectable options through the WhatsApp experience.

Action-plan delivery

Send completed action plans, checklists and relevant links through WhatsApp.

Shared user context

Use the same permitted profile and conversation context across the website and WhatsApp.

Separate promotional consent

Ask separately for permission to send promotional or marketing messages.

09

Public knowledge articles

Features for turning useful guidance into freely accessible resources.

Identify reusable answers

Identify completed explanations that may be useful to a wider audience.

Convert answer to article

Prepare a readable public article from suitable general guidance.

Remove personal information

Remove names, contact details, personal circumstances and identifying conversation details.

Article accuracy review

Check important claims, references and official links before publication.

Publication approval

Require appropriate permission and review before an article is made public.

Free public access

Allow anyone to read published knowledge articles without using a paid query or credit.

10

Account and user controls

Features for managing the user's My Guide experience.

Account access

Allow users to return to their saved profile and permitted history.

My queries

Show previous queries and their completion status.

My action plans

Show completed and saved action plans for future reference.

Saved interests

Show interests and goals that the user has permitted My Guide to retain.

Communication preferences

Let users manage their connected channels and promotional communication choices.

Help and support

Provide access to support for account, query, payment and information issues.

11

Administration and content management

Operational features needed to manage the service.

User management

View and manage user accounts and their service status with appropriate access controls.

Query and outcome review

Review query activity, completion outcomes and user-reported issues.

Pricing and credit configuration

Manage query pricing, free-query allowances and promotional credit rules.

Payment oversight

Review payment records, failed transactions and refund-related requests.

Knowledge and source review

Review source quality, outdated information and reported incorrect guidance.

Public article review

Review, approve, update or unpublish public articles.

Consent and privacy requests

Handle user requests relating to saved information, permissions and deletion.

Usage reporting

View aggregate usage, popular query categories, completed plans and credit usage.
